## Visa and taxation

- The procedure is known as "Work Remotely from Dubai" (Virtual Working Programme) and is managed through the GDRFA of Dubai, without the need for a local sponsor.
- The sources do not agree on the minimum income required, which is between 3,500 and 5,000 dollars per month, so it is advisable to confirm the exact figure before submitting the application (the official Invest in Dubai portal refers to this same program without detailing the threshold, while specialized guides such as Citizen Remote place the requirement at 3,500 dollars per month, accredited with payroll and six months of bank statements).
- Regarding tax residence, the criterion of 183 days (or 90 days under certain conditions) is usually cited, although this information has not been confirmed with an official primary source.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the leading private hospitals for expatriates in Dubai are the American Hospital Dubai, located in Al Barsha, with 254 beds, more than 30 specialties, a comprehensive oncology program and the merit of having performed the first robotic surgery in the city; the Mediclinic group, which operates several locations in the Emirates (including Mediclinic City Hospital) and has more than 950 outpatient beds between cardiology, obstetrics, dermatology and dentistry; and the Saudi German Hospital Dubai, at Al Barsha 3, with 300 beds, 35 outpatient specialties, six operating rooms, two catheterization laboratories and a laboratory accredited by the CAP, also aimed at medical tourism.
- Health insurance is required by law: Dubai Law No. 11 of 2013 requires all residents – including expatriates with work visas, their spouses, children, elderly dependent parents and domestic staff – to have minimum health coverage, usually provided by the employer or sponsor.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Dubai Marina is a social area, with access to the beach and intense nightlife; The annual rent for a studio ranges from AED 80,000 to 110,000, and for a one-bedroom apartment from AED 95,000 to 135,000.
- JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence) is similar to Marina, with a public beach, and its prices are somewhat higher: a studio ranges from AED 85,000 to 115,000 per year, and a room, from AED 100,000 to 140,000.
- Downtown Dubai is the most iconic area and also one of the most expensive: a studio costs between AED 75,000 and 100,000 per year, and a room, between AED 90,000 and 130,000.
- In coworkings, general market prices in Dubai range between AED 50 and 100 per day for a shared position (hot desk) and between AED 800 and 1,500 per month; a dedicated desk costs between AED 1,200 and 2,500 per month; a private office for 2-4 people ranges from AED 300 to 500 per day or AED 5,000 to 10,000 per month; and a meeting room costs between AED 100 and 200 per hour.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Dubai and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
