## Visa and taxation

- The procedure is known as "Work Remotely from Dubai" (Virtual Working Programme) and is managed through the GDRFA of Dubai, without the need for a local sponsor.
- The sources do not agree on the minimum income required, which is between 3,500 and 5,000 dollars per month, so it is advisable to confirm the exact figure before submitting the application (the official Invest in Dubai portal refers to this same program without detailing the threshold, while specialized guides such as Citizen Remote place the requirement at 3,500 dollars per month, accredited with payroll and six months of bank statements).
- The most costly mistake is to assume that "living in Dubai" is enough in itself to stop being a tax resident in Spain, without objectively proving either the 183 days of stay or the transfer of the center of vital interests, especially if the family remains in Spain.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the leading private hospitals for expatriates in Dubai are the American Hospital Dubai, located in Al Barsha, with 254 beds, more than 30 specialties, a comprehensive oncology program and the merit of having performed the first robotic surgery in the city.
- Health insurance is required by law: Dubai Law No. 11 of 2013 requires all residents – including expatriates with work visas, their spouses, children, elderly dependent parents and domestic staff – to have minimum health coverage, usually provided by the employer or sponsor.
- the Mediclinic group, which operates several locations in the Emirates (including Mediclinic City Hospital) and has more than 950 outpatient beds between cardiology, obstetrics, dermatology and dentistry.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Dubai Marina is a social area, with access to the beach and intense nightlife.
- The annual rent for a studio ranges from AED 80,000 to 110,000, and for a one-bedroom apartment, from AED 95,000 to 135,000.
- JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence) is similar to Marina, with a public beach, and its prices are somewhat higher: a studio ranges from AED 85,000 to 115,000 per year, and a room, from AED 100,000 to 140,000.
- In coworkings, general market prices in Dubai range between AED 50 and 100 per day for a hot desk and between AED 800 and 1,500 per month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Dubai and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
