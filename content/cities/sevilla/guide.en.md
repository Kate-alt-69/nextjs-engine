## Visa and taxation

- For a Spanish citizen there is no visa procedure: settling in Seville is simply a change of residence within the country.
- The procedure is carried out at Importass (Social Security) together with the census registration at the Tax Agency (form 036/037).

## Health and medical insurance

- In Seville, public health depends on the Andalusian Health Service (SAS), the regional system that manages primary care, hospitals, emergencies and prevention programs throughout Andalusia, within the framework of the National Health System.
- To access it as a resident you must be registered in the city and apply for the Andalusian Health Card through the SAS portal or the 'Salud Andalucía' app.
- As a complement, there is a wide range of private health insurance in Seville (Sanitas, Adeslas, DKV, Asisa), useful for those looking for shorter waiting lists.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Triana: on the other side of the Guadalquivir, it is the neighborhood with the most flamenco and pottery tradition in Seville, with an authentic and very lively atmosphere.
- Rents are around €700-750 for a one- or two-bedroom apartment, and it offers a good connection on foot to the center by crossing the Triana bridge.
- Alameda de Hércules: the most bohemian and alternative neighborhood in the city, with a high density of bars, terraces and restaurants; It is one of the favorite areas of expats and young digital nomads for its social nightlife and its proximity to the center and several coworkings.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Seville and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
