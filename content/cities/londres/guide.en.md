## Visa and taxation

- Since Brexit, a Spanish citizen no longer has freedom of movement in the United Kingdom and must enter as a standard visitor (Standard Visitor), which allows stays of up to six months without the need for a prior visa, but with a general prohibition on working for a British company or as a self-employed person in the country.
- There is no specific digital nomad visa in the United Kingdom, so anyone who wants to reside there for a long time and work must opt ​​for formal immigration routes.
- For Spanish tax purposes, as long as the stay in the United Kingdom does not exceed 183 calendar days in the year and the center of economic interests does not move, the remote worker remains a tax resident in Spain and must declare his or her income there.

## Health and medical insurance

- A Spanish visitor to the United Kingdom does not have free access to the NHS except in very specific cases: emergency care in A&E is free, as is the treatment of certain infectious diseases, but most hospital care, prescriptions, dentists and opticians are charged separately.
- The NHS expressly recommends that visitors take out private health insurance, as anyone who does not have coverage may face bills equivalent to 150% of the national NHS rate for non-exempt treatments.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Shoreditch and Hoxton, in east London, are the historical epicenter of the creative and technological scene, with a very high density of cafes suitable for work and coworkings such as WeWork, Huckletree or Uncommon.
- One-bedroom rents in the area typically range between GBP 1,600 and GBP 2,700 per month.
- Hackney, just to the north, offers a more residential and affordable environment while still being close to the nightlife and co-working spaces of East London.
- Canary Wharf, the financial district, concentrates corporate offices and coworking spaces aimed at finance and consulting, with quick metro connections to the center, although the atmosphere is more corporate than bohemian.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in London and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
