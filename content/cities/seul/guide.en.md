## Visa and taxation

- The procedure is the F-1-D visa, known as "Workcation", which since June 30, 2026 has become a permanent program (leaving its pilot phase behind) and whose maximum duration has been extended to 3 years.
- In tax matters, from 2026 the 183 continuous days of stay that cross two tax years also give rise to tax residence.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the large hospitals in the city, the Asan Medical Center, Samsung Medical Center and Severance Hospital (Yonsei University) stand out, all three with specific centers for international patients.
- In out-of-pocket expenses, a general consultation without NHIS coverage costs between 30,000 and 60,000 won; a consultation with a specialist, between 30,000 and 150,000 won; an emergency, between 200,000 and 500,000 won; one day of hospitalization, 50,000 to 2.7 million won; and an appendectomy in a private hospital, between 11 and 20 million won (between 6,770 and 12,310 euros).

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Itaewon is an international neighborhood, with good schools, and rents between 900,000 and 1.4 million won per month.
- Hongdae, young and noisy, moves between 700,000 and 1.3 million.
- Yeonnam-dong, quieter and with a cafe atmosphere, ranges between 800,000 and 1.3 million.
- In coworkings, Hoppin House, in Yeonnam-dong, costs $16 a day and $206 a month, with 24/7 access; Hey Ground charges from $275 a month, also 24/7; Local Stitch Creator Town costs $254 per month; Blue Pebbles, the cheapest, charges $11 a day and $130 a month; ECS153, in Gangnam, ranges from $210 to $290 a month; and WeWork has fees starting at $217 per month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Seoul and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
