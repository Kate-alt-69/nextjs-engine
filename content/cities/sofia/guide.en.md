## Visa and taxation

- As a Spanish citizen, you do not need a visa to live in Bulgaria: both countries are members of the European Union and the right of free movement applies directly.
- In tax matters, the general rule for a Spanish citizen who moves to live abroad is that the Treasury considers a tax resident in Spain to be someone who stays more than 183 calendar days in Spanish territory during the calendar year, or someone who has the main core of their economic interests in Spain (directly or indirectly), unless they prove their tax residence in another country.

## Health and medical insurance

- As an EU citizen, the European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) allows you to receive necessary medical assistance in the Bulgarian public network during temporary stays, covering for example consultations with arranged doctors (with a small co-payment) and hospitalization in public centers (also with a reduced daily rate).
- However, the EHIC is designed for specific stays, not for stable residence: as soon as you register as a resident in Bulgaria, your country of origin can cancel the validity of the card for that use.
- The NHIF covers primary and specialist care consultations (with referral), hospitalization, subsidized medications and maternal and child care.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Lozenets is the favorite neighborhood of many digital nomads: a residential area with tree-lined streets near the center, with a good offer of cafes and work spaces, where a one-bedroom apartment costs between 600 and 900 euros per month.
- Oborishte, also central, has a more artistic and cultural atmosphere, with galleries and cafes, and somewhat more affordable rents, between 500 and 800 euros.
- For those looking for cheaper options, Studentski Grad (the 'Student City') offers apartments from 350-550 euros per month in a young and lively atmosphere, while Mladost, a residential district somewhat further away but well connected by metro, has similar rents, between 350 and 600 euros.
- In terms of coworking spaces, Betahaus Sofia is the most consolidated network, with four locations spread across the city (Shipka, Slaveykov Square, ABC and Sofia Tower): it offers a day bonus for 20 euros, a 'Light' shared desk plan with 24/5 access for 85 euros per month, a fixed position ('Open Space') from 200 euros per month and a private office from 250 euros per month per person (prices without VAT).

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Sofia and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
