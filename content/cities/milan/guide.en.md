## Visa and taxation

- As a Spanish citizen, you enjoy free movement within the EU and can enter, reside and work in Italy without a visa.
- Many digital nomads opt for the forfettario regime, a simplified tax regime with a reduced tax rate of 5% during the first 5 years of activity (15% thereafter), applicable up to an annual income threshold of €85,000 (limit also in force in 2026).

## Health and medical insurance

- Italy has the Servizio Sanitario Nazionale (SSN), a universal public system financed by taxes.
- EU citizens, including Spaniards, can register for the SSN for free if they prove residence and fit into recognized categories: employed or self-employed worker, family member of a worker or Italian citizen, or permanent resident after 5 years.
- For those who do not yet meet the mandatory registration requirements, there is the option of voluntary registration by paying an annual fee, or contracting complementary private insurance.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Navigli, with its iconic canals, is the most popular neighborhood among expats and digital nomads: quiet atmosphere by day, intense nightlife at dusk, terraces and antique market.
- Studios of about 45 m² cost around €650-800/month plus expenses.
- Isola is the creative and innovative hub of Milan, with boutiques, second-hand stores and the Santeria cultural space.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Milan and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
