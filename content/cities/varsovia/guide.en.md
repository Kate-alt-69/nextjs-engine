## Visa and taxation

- For a Spanish citizen, as they belong to the European Union, free movement applies.
- For citizens from outside the European Union there is no official digital nomad visa in Poland.
- Among the most frequent errors are choosing the wrong tax regime without professional advice, underestimating ZUS (Polish social security) contributions and assuming that the IP Box requirements are met without verifying it.

## Health and medical insurance

- Major private clinics include Medicover (which includes the American Clinic Warsaw) and LUX MED.
- In out-of-pocket expenses, a consultation with the general practitioner costs between 150 and 300 PLN, a consultation with a specialist between 200 and 450 PLN, and a hospitalization without insurance can cost "several hundred to several thousand" PLN.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Among the city's neighborhoods, Śródmieście (the center, expensive), Stare Miasto (the historic center), Wola (modern and with a better price than the center), Mokotów (exclusive and with good value for money), Prague (bohemian, the cheapest and in the middle of a transition) and Żoliborz (green and economical) stand out.
- As a reference, a room in the center costs about 1,050 euros per month, and outside the center about 835 euros per month.
- In terms of coworkings, Mindspace (premium range), Regus (serviced offices), Business Link (a historic network in the city) and Spaces, belonging to the IWG group, stand out.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Warsaw and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
