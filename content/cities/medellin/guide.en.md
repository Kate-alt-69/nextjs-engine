## Visa and taxation

- The corresponding procedure is the Visa V — Digital Nomad, which is requested through the SITAC platform and, once granted, is formalized with the immigration card before Migration Colombia.
- The most common errors are believing that living outside of Colombia automatically eliminates the tax obligation when the center of economic and vital interests continues to be in the country, and confusing the civil domicile with the tax domicile.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the reference hospital centers, the Pablo Tobón Uribe Hospital stands out, which is among the 250 best in the world according to a ranking by an American magazine, and the Las Américas Clinic, which has an international care office.
- The out-of-pocket expense per consultation ranges between 35,000 and 250,000 COP, and joining the EPS costs approximately 110,000 COP per month.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- El Poblado is the most international neighborhood and also the most expensive, with rents between 850 and 1,200 dollars per month.
- Laureles offers a more authentic and university atmosphere, with prices between $540 and $730, between 30% and 40% cheaper than El Poblado.
- Envigado is quiet and familiar, with rents between 560 and 855 dollars.
- As for coworkings, Selina Medellín charges 10 dollars a day and between 75 and 120 dollars a month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Medellín and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
