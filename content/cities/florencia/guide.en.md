## Visa and taxation

- European Union citizens do not need a visa to live and work in Florence thanks to free movement, and only need to register with the municipality if they stay for more than three months.
- Non-EU citizens can apply for the visa per nomadi digitali, the Italian digital nomad visa, which requires proof of annual income from remote work of at least three times the health exemption threshold (between €24,789 and €25,500 approximately depending on the consulate), professional qualifications and a minimum of six months of demonstrable previous experience.
- In tax matters, anyone who moves their residence to Italy and stays more than 183 days a year in the country can benefit from the impatriate workers regime, which offers a 50% exemption on taxable income for up to five years for those who meet the qualification requirements and effective transfer of residence.

## Health and medical insurance

- Italy has a public health system, the Servizio Sanitario Nazionale, to which non-EU citizens with a residence permit have the right to register with the ASL of their district, with access to a family doctor and hospital care under the same conditions as Italian residents.
- However, to obtain the digital nomad visa it is mandatory to previously present private medical insurance valid throughout the Italian territory that covers treatment and hospitalization during the requested period of stay.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Santo Spirito, in the Oltrarno neighborhood on the other side of the Arno River, is the favorite area of digital nomads and expatriates for its artisanal and less touristy atmosphere, with one-bedroom rentals between €900 and €1,500 per month.
- San Niccolò-Porta Romana, also in Oltrarno, offers a similar atmosphere with views of the Fiesole hills, between €1,100 and €2,200.
- The historic center has the highest prices in the city, while Campo di Marte, more residential and with better connection to the airport, is more affordable for those seeking tranquility.
- Among the coworkings, Nana Bianca, next to the river a few minutes from the Santa Maria Novella station, is the largest in the city with 6,500 m² aimed at digital creators and startups.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Florence and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
