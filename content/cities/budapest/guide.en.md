## Visa and taxation

- The procedure for digital nomads in Hungary is the White Card, managed by the OIF (National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing).
- The most common errors are believing that the White Card automatically exempts you from paying taxes in Hungary, and not correctly closing your tax residence in Spain before moving.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the leading private clinics in Budapest, FirstMed Centers, Medicover Hospital and Duna Medical Center stand out.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Among the most recommended neighborhoods for digital nomads in Budapest is District VII, known for its nightlife, with rents of around 600 euros per month for a 45 m² apartment.
- District V, elegant and next to the Danube, from 800 euros per month.
- District VIII, more authentic and between 25% and 30% cheaper (from about 450 euros per month).
- As for coworkings, Kaptár (about 8 euros per day or 100 euros per month), KUBIK (about 12 euros per day, with terrace), ClickOffice (private offices from about 15 euros per hour), Espresso Embassy (a coffee costs about 2.50 euros), Kelet Café (with its "Remote Worker Menu" for about 8 euros) and the Szabó Ervin Library (about 5 euros) stand out. per day).

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Budapest and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
