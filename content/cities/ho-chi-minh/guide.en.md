## Visa and taxation

- Vietnam does not have a digital nomad visa.
- The third way is the SVEC, aimed at highly talented profiles, inaccessible to the majority of digital nomads.
- The most common mistakes are not counting arrival and departure days as full days, ignoring the rental rule, and simply assuming that physically working from Vietnam for foreign clients never constitutes "Vietnamese source income", a criterion that is in fact the subject of debate.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the leading private hospitals in Ho Chi Minh are FV Hospital (District 7), Vinmec Central Park (Binh Thanh) and Family Medical Practice (Diamond Plaza).
- International hospitals usually require a deposit from those who do not have insurance.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Among the most common areas for digital nomads are District 1 (the center, between 500 and 800 dollars per month), Thảo Điền, in District 2 (expatriate profile, between 400 and 700 dollars per month, 20-30 minutes from the center), District 3 (colonial style, between 350 and 550 dollars per month), Bình Thạnh (modern, between $300 and $500 a month), Phú Nhuận (more local and cheaper) and Phú Mỹ Hưng, in District 7 (a gated community with international schools, from about $400 a month).
- In coworkings, Dreamplex stands out (with monthly subscriptions of between 60 and 120 dollars), The Hive (also between 60 and 120 dollars per month, with a strong community component), Toong (present in several cities), Cafe Apartment, in District 1 (between 2 and 4 dollars per drink) and the Phúc Long and The Coffee House chains (with a connection between 80 and 150 Mbps).

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Ho Chi Minh and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
