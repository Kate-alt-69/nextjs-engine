## Visa and taxation

- Spanish citizens can enter and stay in Serbia without a visa for a maximum of 90 days within any 180-day period, either with a passport or with an ID, as confirmed by the Serbian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
- Serbia does not have an official 'digital nomad visa', but there is a practical and widely used route for foreign freelancers: registering as a 'preduzetnik paušalac' (individual entrepreneur with flat-rate taxation) with the Commercial Registry Agency (APR), which allows you to then apply for a temporary residence permit renewable annually (up to 3 years of continuous residence before being eligible for permanent residence).
- For Spanish tax purposes, it is worth remembering the general rule: a person is considered to be a tax resident in Spain if they remain in Spanish territory for more than 183 days within a calendar year, or if the main nucleus or base of their activities or economic interests is located in Spain, directly or indirectly.

## Health and medical insurance

- The Serbian public health system (RFZO) is only fully accessible to citizens, legal residents and people who contribute to the health insurance fund (for example, through the 'preduzetnik paušalac' scheme, which includes a health insurance fee within the monthly fixed contribution).
- A tourist or someone without legal residence cannot, in general, access the public system for free, so it is strongly recommended to take out international private health insurance before traveling or settling in Belgrade.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Vračar is the 'old-fashioned' residential neighborhood for expatriates and diplomats: quiet streets, good gastronomic offerings and proximity to the center, with one-bedroom rents that usually range between €500 and €1,100 per month, and even more on the most sought-after streets.
- Dorćol and Stari Grad (historic center, next to the pedestrian Knez Mihailova) combine cultural atmosphere, cafes and nightlife with a completely walkable location, in a similar or slightly higher price range.
- Savamala, the old port area converted into a creative neighborhood with industrial warehouses, galleries and craft breweries, is one of the favorite areas of the nomadic community for its young atmosphere and coworkings.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Belgrade and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
