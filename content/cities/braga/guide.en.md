## Visa and taxation

- As a Spanish citizen, Braga—and Portugal in general—does not require a visa: EU free movement allows you to enter and reside without prior formalities.
- For non-EU nomads, Portugal offers the D8 visa (digital nomad visa), which in 2026 requires proof of a minimum monthly income of €3,680 (four times the Portuguese minimum wage, set at €920/month from January 2026).
- In tax matters, the old Non-Habitual Resident (RNH) regime was replaced in 2024 by the IFICI (Incentivo Fiscal à Investigação Cientifica e Inovação), which offers a fixed rate of 20% on income from work and professional activity of high added value obtained in Portugal for up to 10 years, as long as the applicant has not been a Portuguese tax resident in the previous 5 years and works in recognized activities (R&D, ICT, engineering, university teaching, certified startups, among others).

## Health and medical insurance

- Once registered as a resident (with the Registration Certificate for community members, or the residence permit for D8 holders), it is possible to register with the National Health Service (SNS) by going to the corresponding health center and requesting the 'user number', which gives access to Portuguese public health under similar conditions to a national resident.
- D8 visa applicants must also take out, before the consular appointment, private medical insurance with a minimum coverage of €30,000 valid in the EU/Schengen Area (including medical evacuation and repatriation); This requirement disappears once residency and access to the SNS is obtained, although many residents choose to maintain complementary private insurance.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- The historic center, the civil area known as Sé e Ctividade (around the Braga Cathedral), is the most comfortable option for nomads: everything is done on foot, there is a good offer of cafes and fiber optics, and 1-bedroom apartments in the center cost around €550/month (compared to around €442/month in areas somewhat further from the center, according to cost of living data based on Numbeo).
- Maximinos, adjacent to the historic center and very close to the university, is a popular area among students and nomads due to its young atmosphere and somewhat lower prices.
- Whoever prefers to be close to the Gualtar campus of the Universidade do Minho will find cheaper rentals and good urban transportation connections to the center.
- In coworkings, Braga has several options: Regus (Avenida da Liberdade), with coworking from €159/month and private office from €195/month; Braga Business Center, with positions from €140/month; Liberty Hub Coworking, from €110/month; and Factory Braga, with coworking from €102/month and private office from €274/month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Braga and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
