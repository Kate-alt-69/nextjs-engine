## Visa and taxation

- The main procedure is the DE Rantau Nomad Pass, managed by the Malaysia Digital Economic Corporation (MDEC).
- Malaysia does not have a special tax regime for digital nomads.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the reference hospitals for international patients, the Gleneagles Kuala Lumpur and the Prince Court Medical Center stand out, both specifically aimed at caring for foreigners.
- In out-of-pocket expenses, a consultation in the public system costs between RM40 and 100, in the private system between RM80 and 250, and in an international hospital between RM250 and 600.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Among the most prominent neighborhoods in Kuala Lumpur is KLCC, the city's business center, with rents ranging from RM 1,800-3,500 in the mid-range to RM 4,500-8,000 in the premium range.
- Bukit Bintang, known for its intense nightlife, has prices from RM 1,800-3,500.
- Bangsar, popular with expats for its brunch atmosphere, fetches RM4,500-7,500.
- As for coworking spaces, Colony @ KLCC costs $9 per day or $107 per month and includes a pool; WORQ Bangsar offers a daily pass for $9 and a monthly pass for $111, with 24/7 access; WORQ Intermark charges $16 per day and $133 per month, also with 24/7 access; Common Ground KL Eco City has a daily pass of $11 and monthly pass of $178; WORQ KL Sentral offers $13 per day and $133 per month with 24/7 access; and Komune @ KLCC costs $11 per day and $111 per month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Kuala Lumpur and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
