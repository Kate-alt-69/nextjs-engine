## Visa and taxation

- Spanish citizens can enter South Africa without a visa as tourists or business visitors for a stay of up to 90 days, according to the list of visa-exempt countries from the South African Department of Home Affairs.
- For longer stays aimed at remote work, South Africa introduced a 'Remote Work Visa' (under section 11(1)(b)(iv) of the Immigration Act), published in regulations in October 2024 and with applications operational from March 2025.
- For tax purposes, anyone who stays more than 183 days a year in Spain or maintains the center of their economic interests there is still considered a Spanish tax resident and must declare their worldwide income to the Tax Agency regardless of where they physically work.

## Health and medical insurance

- South Africa has a clearly divided healthcare system: public healthcare is underfunded and saturated, with overcrowded centers and long waiting lists, while the private sector – used by only around 16% of the population but which concentrates around 60% of the country's doctors – offers much shorter waiting times and more modern facilities.
- Therefore, it is strongly recommended to take out private or international health insurance before settling in Cape Town.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- The most popular neighborhoods for digital nomads in Cape Town include Sea Point, a very walkable area next to the seafront with one-bedroom rentals between ZAR 8,000 and ZAR 10,000 per month.
- the City Bowl/CBD, the central area with the highest concentration of coworking and nightlife, with one-bedroom apartments between ZAR 7,000 and 9,000 per month.
- Woodstock, a more creative and local neighborhood with rents starting at around ZAR 8,000 per month for a bedroom.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Cape Town and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
