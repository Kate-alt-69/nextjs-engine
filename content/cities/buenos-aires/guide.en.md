## Visa and taxation

- The specific procedure is the Transitional Residence as a Digital Nomad, managed by the National Directorate of Migration (DNM) under article 24h of Law 25,871, and is only available for nationalities that do not already have a tourist visa.
- unlike digital nomad residence, it does contemplate family reunification (spouse, parents and children).
- In tax matters, you are a resident if you have permanent immigration residence, if you have stayed more than 12 months in the country, or if you exceed 183 days with a center of interest in Argentina.

## Health and medical insurance

- Among the reference hospitals in Buenos Aires are the Italian Hospital of Buenos Aires, the German Hospital and the Austral University Hospital, in Pilar.
- There is no reliable data on the cost of an emergency or hospitalization, so it is recommended to have insurance from the first day.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Among the most recommended neighborhoods are Palermo (the one that offers the greatest selection, also the most expensive), Recoleta (elegant, with similar or slightly higher prices), Belgrano (family friendly, with access to subway line D), San Telmo and Monserrat (bohemian and cheaper), Villa Crespo (between 20% and 40% cheaper than Palermo) and Almagro/Caballito (with average prices and a more discreet profile).
- Regarding the airports, the journey to Ezeiza from these neighborhoods is between 30 and 50 minutes, while Aeroparque is much closer.
- In coworkings, with prices usually expressed in dollars as it is the reference currency of the sector, AreaTres (in Palermo Soho, about 28 dollars a day, with rooms from 152 dollars a day), Huerta Cowork (about 20 dollars a day, B Corp certified), La Maquinita Co.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Buenos Aires and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
