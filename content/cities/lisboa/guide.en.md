## Visa and taxation

- The main procedure is the D8 visa, which is carried out in two phases: the first before the Portuguese consulate of the country of residence, and the second - already in Portugal - before the Agency for Integration, Migrations and Asylum (AIMA).
- Among the most frequent errors are believing that the D8 visa gives automatic access to the IFICI, not processing the NIF and NISS on time, and ignoring that exceeding 183 days of stay requires declaring worldwide income.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Lisbon and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
