## Visa and taxation

- Citizens of the European Union and the European Economic Area do not need a visa or work permit to settle in Barcelona: free movement allows them to reside and work without any further procedure than registering in the municipal registry and requesting the NIE.
- For digital nomads from outside the EU, since 2023 Spain offers the same visa for international teleworkers as in the rest of the country, with a monthly income requirement of around €2,849/month in 2026 and an initial validity of three years, extendable for periods of two.
- For the rest of taxpayers, the general rule of tax residence applies: more than 183 days a year in the country.

## Health and medical insurance

- Legal residents in Barcelona register their health card with CatSalut, the public health service of Catalonia integrated into the National Health System, with free or highly subsidized coverage once affiliation to Social Security is formalized.
- However, the international teleworker visa requires taking out private medical insurance with full coverage in Spain before requesting authorization, since access to the public system is not guaranteed from the first day of the procedure.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Gràcia is the favorite bohemian neighborhood of the digital nomad community, with pedestrian plazas and a village atmosphere within the city.
- A one-bedroom rental is between €600 and €1,200/month.
- El Born, in the old town, combines medieval architecture with intense social life and similar or slightly higher prices.
- Among the coworkings, Betahaus Barcelona stands out, a pioneer in the sector since 2010, located in Gràcia, with flexible positions from €150/month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Barcelona and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
