## Visa and taxation

- The main procedure is the Residence Authorization for Teleworking of an International Character, regulated by article 74 bis of Law 14/2013, which can be requested before the Large Companies and Strategic Groups Unit (UGE-CE) if the applicant is already in Spain, or before the Spanish consulate corresponding to the country of residence if it is processed from abroad.
- Employed workers must provide a contract with a minimum tenure of three months, a letter from the employer that expressly authorizes teleworking, the corresponding payroll and form A1 or registration in the General Regime of Spanish Social Security.
- The special regime known as the Beckham Law allows taxation at a fixed rate of 24% on the first €600,000 of earned income, with exemption for income obtained outside of Spain for six years.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Valencia and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.
