## Visa and taxation

- A Spanish citizen can enter Sri Lanka with the electronic travel authorization (ETA), free from 2026 for more than 40 nationalities, including Spain, and which allows an initial stay of up to 30 days with two entries within that period.
- For a more stable stay, Sri Lanka introduced a digital nomad visa in 2026 that requires a minimum income of $2,000 per month from foreign sources, a criminal record certificate, international health insurance and a fee of $500 per person.
- In tax matters, neither the ETA nor the digital nomad visa exempts you from paying taxes in Spain: anyone who spends more than 183 days a year in Spanish territory, or maintains the center of their economic interests there, continues to be considered a Spanish tax resident and must declare their worldwide income to the Tax Agency regardless of where they physically work.

## Health and medical insurance

- Sri Lanka's public system offers free care to its citizens but has long waiting lists for specialized services, which is why resident foreigners almost universally resort to private healthcare, concentrated above all in Colombo.
- Hospitals such as Nawaloka Hospital, Asiri Health and Lanka Hospitals have English-speaking staff, foreign-trained specialists and standards of care comparable to Western ones in major specialties, although quality drops markedly outside the capital and large cities.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Colombo 7 (Cinnamon Gardens) is the most prestigious neighborhood in the city, with tree-lined streets, colonial-era mansions, the best private schools and hospitals and coworkings such as Catalyst by Hub9 or The Office Colombo, but also the highest rents.
- Colombo 5 (Havelock Town) is the preferred area for a large part of the expatriate community due to its central location, its mix of modern apartments and colonial houses, its parks and international schools, and is home to spaces such as Worx Jawatte House or CO-LABs, with coworking fees that range from about 90 to more than 200 dollars per month depending on the services included.
- Colombo 3 (Kollupitiya), next to the Galle Face Green seafront, is the most cosmopolitan option with more nightlife, while Colombo 6 (Wellawatte) offers a more affordable alternative with a coastal atmosphere and a good range of restaurants.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Colombo and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
