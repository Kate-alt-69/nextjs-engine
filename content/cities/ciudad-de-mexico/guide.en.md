## Visa and taxation

- Mexico does not have an official "digital nomad visa"; The applicable procedure is the Temporary Resident Visa, which is requested at the corresponding Mexican consulate (through the SRE/MiConsulate system) and which, once obtained, is exchanged at the National Migration Institute (INM).
- At the tax level, you are a resident if you stay in Mexico for 183 days or if your center of vital interests is located in the country.

## Health and medical insurance

- Reference hospitals include Médica Sur, accredited by the JCI and ranked number 1 in the Newsweek ranking of 2024, in addition to being affiliated with the Mayo Clinic; the ABC Medical Center, also accredited by JCI and distinguished in cardiology and oncology; and Hospital Ángeles, the largest private hospital chain in the country.
- As a free alternative, since July 2024 there is IMSS-Wellbeing for people not affiliated with the social security system.
- The out-of-pocket expense for a private consultation ranges between $300 and $600 MXN.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Roma Norte is a social and very walkable neighborhood, with rents between $900 and $1,600 per month.
- Condesa is quieter and more wooded, with prices between 1,000 and 1,800 dollars.
- Polanco is the premium and corporate area, with rents between 1,400 and 3,500 dollars.
- Among coworkings, Colony, in Roma Norte, charges between $400 and $500 MXN per day and between $3,500 and $4,500 MXN per month; Impact Hub CDMX has a monthly fee of approximately $3,000 MXN; PÚBLICO offers 24-hour access with a monthly payment of between $3,000 and $3,500 MXN; Spacioss, in Narvarte, charges between $2,900 and $3,200 MXN per month and stands out for its good value for money; WeWork Polanco has monthly plans starting at $3,459 MXN; and Co-Madre Roma, founded by women, charges $3,500 MXN per month.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Mexico City and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

## Data sources

- Numbeo Cost of Living Index (without rent), calculated using New York as a reference and converted to euros at the current exchange rate.
- Average fixed broadband speed by country, Speedtest Global Index (Ookla).
- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
- Numbeo Quality of Life Index (combines purchasing power, health, climate, cost, traffic and pollution), rescaled to a score out of 10.
