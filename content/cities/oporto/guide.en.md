## Visa and taxation

- Citizens of the European Union and the European Economic Area can settle in Porto without any visa procedure, protected by the free movement of people; It is enough to register with the local authorities after three months of residence.
- For non-EU digital nomads, Portugal offers the D8 visa, which requires proof of monthly income of at least €3,680 (four times the Portuguese minimum wage), demonstrated by payroll, contract and real bank statements.
- For the rest of taxpayers, the general rule of tax residence applies: anyone who stays more than 183 days a year in the country is considered a resident in Portugal.

## Health and medical insurance

- Legal residents in Portugal have access to the National Health Service (SNS) once they have formalized their registration at the health center in their area, with low-cost public care for the patient.
- However, to process the D8 visa it is mandatory to present private medical insurance with a minimum coverage of €30,000 that covers treatment and hospitalization in Portugal during the entire application period.

## Neighborhoods and coworkings

- Cedofeita is the reference neighborhood for young digital nomads due to its bohemian atmosphere, its art galleries and its high density of cafes with Wi-Fi; A one-bedroom apartment is between €700 and €950 per month.
- Bonfim, more residential and somewhat more affordable, attracts those seeking tranquility without moving away from the center, with rents from €650 for a bedroom.
- Foz do Douro, next to the mouth of the river, is the most sought-after area due to its proximity to the beach, with rents exceeding €1,100 per month, while Baixa, the historic center, combines tourism and local life with intermediate prices, around €900 for a bedroom.
- Among the coworkings, Porto i/o stands out, one of the most consolidated spaces in the city, with flexible positions and an active community of startups; Selina Navis, which combines coworking and coliving next to the Douro River; and Vertical Coworking, in the center, aimed at freelancers and small teams with meeting rooms and 24/7 access.

## Internet and mobile data

- Activate an eSIM before arriving in Porto and have data from minute one, without depending on the airport Wi-Fi or looking for a physical SIM.
- It is the most practical option for most nomads: it is activated from the mobile phone before even leaving home, without queues or paperwork upon arrival.

- Numbeo Safety Index (residents' perception of safety), rescaled to a score out of 10.
